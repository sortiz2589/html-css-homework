Welcome to FC Barcelona

FC Barcelona is one of the most recognized football clubs in the world. Founded in 1899, the club represents more than football. Its history, supporters, and style of play have made it an important part of the sport.

From the energy of the supporters to the traditions of the club, Barcelona continues to inspire generations of football fans around the world.
