About

Spider-Man is the superhero identity of Peter Parker. He is known for helping people, protecting his neighborhood, and trying to do the right thing even when life gets complicated. His story shows that being a hero is about more than having powers.

The city is where Spider-Man looks out for his community.

Characteristics

  • Brave when others need help
  • Creative and quick-thinking
  • Funny, even in stressful moments
  • Loyal to friends and family
  • Responsible for how he uses his abilities

Friends

  • Mary Jane Watson
  • Gwen Stacy
  • Ned Leeds
  • Aunt May
